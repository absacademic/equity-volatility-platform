# Data and research disclaimer

This repository is educational research software, and the author makes no guarantee about its production, investment, or trading efficacy. 

The included datasets are synthetic. They are designed to test point-in-time logic, option selection, execution accounting, hedging, attribution, and reproducibility. Synthetic performance must not be described as real-market evidence.

Users are responsible for obtaining market and event data legally and for following vendor redistribution terms. Historical options data can contain stale quotes, crossed markets, adjusted contracts, corporate-action effects, survivorship bias, timestamp errors, and missing series. Index and ETF products also differ in exercise style, settlement, tax treatment, dividends, and hedge instruments.

Backtests do not guarantee future results. Live execution may differ because of latency, queue position, partial fills, market impact, margin changes, borrow constraints, trading halts, model error, and operational risk.
